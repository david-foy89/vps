Service

Support from the company that sold it

VPS is the sales and service representative in the territory. SureFire builds the equipment. When a unit in Texas, Oklahoma, or southern New Mexico needs a part, a startup, or a straight answer, the call comes here.

Equipment selection

Matching a SureFire controller, ignition unit, or compressor package to the vessel, the burner size, and the rule you are working under.

Startup help

VPS starts SureFire equipment up on location in Texas, Oklahoma, and southern New Mexico. Call or send the service form to schedule it.

Parts

Flame rods, thermocouples, igniters, valves, and the other SureFire service kits listed on the components page.

Troubleshooting

If a unit will not light, call VPS. Phone support is available 24/7, and a technician can come to the site inside the territory.

How to request service

  1. Call (830) 328-1411, email mickey-vps@outlook.com, or send the quote form and choose “Service or repair.”
  2. Give the state and county, the SureFire model if it is on the tag, and what the unit is doing — or not doing.
  3. A photo of the controller face and the status code saves a round of questions.

Quote within 24 hours

Send the equipment and the state. VPS returns a SureFire quote within 24 hours.

In-stock assemblies

In-stock SureFire assemblies ship in 3–5 business days after the order is received.

24/7 customer support

Customer support is available around the clock for equipment VPS supplied in the territory.

Try Before You Buy

Ask VPS to send a unit for a 30-day trial. If it is not the right fit, return it. Outbound shipping is covered. Return shipping is the customer’s.

Office hours: Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM. Support calls are answered 24/7.

Call for service

(830) 328-1411. If you are not sure the site is covered, say the county first.
